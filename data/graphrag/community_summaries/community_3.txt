The main research area is the integration of generative AI in visual content marketing, with a focus on its implications for the future of marketing. Common themes include the importance of visual content marketing in today's digital landscape, its impact on brand recognition and consumer engagement, and the future of visual marketing with emphasis on personalization, real-time operations, and data-driven decisions. Key methods involve a literature review and presentation of statistical data and studies. Important findings highlight the significant role of visual content marketing and the potential of generative AI to transform visual marketing by enabling personalized and data-driven strategies. The integration of AI and visual creativity can lead to innovative and impactful marketing campaigns.
